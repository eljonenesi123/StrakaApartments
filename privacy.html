Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This page explains what information Straka Apartments collects through this website, why, and how it's used. This site is a simple listing page for a single short-term rental apartment in Tirana, Albania — it does not run ads, sell data, or track visitors for marketing purposes.

Information we collect

This site itself does not have a booking form, account system, or database. It doesn't collect or store any personal information directly. The only ways information reaches us are:

  • Email or phone contact — if you email strakaj00@gmail.com, call, or message +355 68 607 1088 on WhatsApp, we receive whatever you send us (name, contact details, message content) directly through that service, not through this website.
  • Booking.com — when you click "Check availability," you're taken to our listing on Booking.com, where any booking, payment, or account information you provide is collected and handled by Booking.com under its own privacy policy, not ours.

Cookies and embedded content

This site loads a small number of third-party resources that may set cookies or collect basic technical data (such as your IP address) in your browser:

  • Google Fonts — used to display the site's typefaces.
  • Google Maps — embedded on the Location section to show the apartment's neighborhood.

We don't control what these third parties do with that data. You can review Google's Privacy Policy for details, or use a browser extension/privacy mode to block third-party embeds if you prefer.

This site does not use its own analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies. Your selected language (EN/SQ/DE) is remembered in your browser's local storage purely so the site loads in the right language next time — this stays on your device and is never sent to us.

How information is used

Any message you send us directly (email, phone, WhatsApp) is used only to respond to your inquiry or manage your stay. We do not share it with third parties beyond what's needed to fulfil a booking (for example, coordinating with Booking.com if you booked through them).

Your rights

If you've contacted us directly and want your information deleted or corrected, email strakaj00@gmail.com and we'll take care of it. If you booked through Booking.com, requests about that data should go to Booking.com directly, since they control that record.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to strakaj00@gmail.com or WhatsApp.
